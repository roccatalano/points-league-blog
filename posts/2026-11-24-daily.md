## The big story: a six-player swap

**Big Herro Glick** and **Spicy town Mayo** pulled off a six-player trade. The transaction log shows Jeremiah Fears, Nic Claxton and Keyonte George on one side, and Austin Reaves, Caleb Martin and Onyeka Okongwu on the other. The injury report has Reaves (41.2 FPTS per game) with Mayo and Claxton (27.9) with Glick.

Reaves is on Mayo's IR with a heel injury, projected back around 27 November, so Mayo took on a star who isn't playing yet. Glick then added Jusuf Nurkic (foot, projected out about a week) and dropped Kingston Flemings. Mayo picked up Collin Gillespie. A full trade review will follow once the numbers are in.
