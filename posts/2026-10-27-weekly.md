## Standings after week 1

Seven teams are 1-0. Ant Social Behaviour leads on points for (1,207), ahead of coachcainzey (1,201), NAWSectual (1,182) and Joseph's Scary Team (1,176). Downtown Euros' 1,118 was the most points by any losing team.

## Awards

- **Upset of the week:** **Big Herro Glick** opened at **$2.75** (about a 30% chance) and beat Rye RSL 985–971.
- **Biggest comeback and biggest choke, all in one game:** on the final morning of the week, Big Herro Glick were down to **13%** and Rye RSL were **87%** to win. Sunday happened. Rye RSL lost by 14. 💀
- **Luck ladder:** Kuminga Lot, Big Herro Glick and Joseph's Scary Team all won as roughly 30% shots, so each is +0.7 wins ahead of what the opening odds expected.
- **Team of the week:** Ant Social Behaviour, 1,207 points, the week's highest score.
- **MVP:** **Tyrese Maxey** (Kream team) put up **200 fantasy points** in 4 games: 115 points, 19 assists, 8 steals and 9 threes. Somehow Kream team still lost by 197. 🔥
- **Bad beat:** Rye RSL lost to Big Herro Glick by **14 points**, 971 to 985.
- **Fewest points in a win:** Big Herro Glick won with 985, and owes Rye RSL a thank-you card.
- **Most points in a loss:** Downtown Euros, 1,118 against coachcainzey's 1,201.
- **Bench points:** NAWSectual left 118 on the bench and still won. The biggest single bench game was **Moussa Diabate**'s 46 for Big Herro Glick (17 points, 8 boards, 5 steals), on a week they won by 14.
- **Best pickup:** **Zaccharie Risacher** gave Ant Social Behaviour 88 points in 3 games (29.3 a game).
- **Worst pickup:** **Anfernee Simons** scored 41 in 4 games for No Scrubs, shooting 8 for 31. No Scrubs lost by 37.
