*The blog launched in week 3, so this review was written after the fact. It uses what we know now, not odds from the time of the trade.*

The ink on the draft was barely dry when **Kream team** and **coachcainzey** got a deal done. It was a straight one-for-one on opening night.

## The deal

- **Kream team** got **Brice Sensabaugh** (UTA). ESPN projected him for 20.6 fantasy points a game before the season, after 22.8 last season.
- **coachcainzey** got **Tre Jones** (CHI). His preseason projection was 25.8 a game, after 31.6 last season.

## The case for each side

**Kream team** was betting on upside: Sensabaugh is a scorer, and scorers can pop. **coachcainzey** took the safer floor: Jones carried the higher projection *and* the better track record. On paper, coachcainzey got about 5 more points a game.

## How it played out

The tracker counts what each player scored in his new team's **starting lineup** from the day after the trade:

- **Tre Jones**: 4 starts, **76** points for coachcainzey
- **Brice Sensabaugh**: 4 starts, **50** points for Kream team

That's **+26 to coachcainzey**. Neither stuck around: Sensabaugh is now a free agent (14.3 a game this season), and Jones has since landed on **Rye RSL** (22.3 a game). The trade is frozen at 76–50.

## Verdict

A small trade that went the way the projections said it would. Kream team bought the lottery ticket and it didn't hit. It hardly mattered, though: Kream team is first in the power rankings anyway.

**coachcainzey: B+** · **Kream team: D+**
