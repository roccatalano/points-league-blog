Two of the model's top four teams did business in week 2.

## The deal

- **Joseph's Scary Team** get **Kyrie Irving** (43.5 fantasy points a game) and **Collin Murray-Boyles** (23.2).
- **coachcainzey** get **Josh Giddey** (40.1) and **Khaman Maluach** (21.3).

## The case for each side

On paper it's close: about 67 points a game going to Joseph's Scary Team and 61 going back. **Joseph's Scary Team** get the best player in the deal, a 24-points-a-night scorer. **coachcainzey** get Giddey's near-triple-double profile (15.5 points, 7.9 boards, 9.0 assists) and a young big in Maluach, who blocks 1.7 a game.

The catch for Joseph's Scary Team: Kyrie is listed day-to-day with a left knee issue and has been ruled out of Monday's game. He's projected back on Wednesday 4 November.

## How it's going

In the first three days, counting only games each player started for his new team:

- **Joseph's Scary Team: 103.** That's Kyrie's 63 in his only start, plus Murray-Boyles' 40 in two.
- **coachcainzey: 33.** That's Giddey's 20 and Maluach's 13.

## The model's view

Rest of season, with today's rosters and injuries:

- **Joseph's Scary Team: +0.20 expected wins**, about 13 more fantasy points a week
- **coachcainzey: −0.14 expected wins**, about 11 fewer a week

## Verdict

A sensible deal that tilts toward the team that got the best player, as long as Kyrie's knee behaves. coachcainzey took on Giddey's floor but gave up the ceiling.

**Joseph's Scary Team: B+** · **coachcainzey: C+**
